Origin
The house he was born into ran on one rule: every child would take up an art. His came through his father's hands — cellist, conductor and luthier in Trujillo.

The line begins with his grandfather, an Italian clarinettist and tailor who emigrated to Venezuela after the Second World War. Santo Scala Bastidas, his father and first teacher, set the discipline of his technique in childhood. By fifteen he was principal cellist of his home state's orchestra — and gave his first lessons the same year.

The four strings

Four callings tuned on the same instrument.

Pluck a string to open it. Each one carries a part of the work — and none of them sounds alone.

Principal at fifteen in the orchestra of his home state. From 2017 to 2020 he played in the Simón Bolívar Symphony Orchestra of Venezuela — the summit of El Sistema — under Gustavo Dudamel, Diego Matheus and Dick van Gasteren, and led the cello section through an opera gala with the country's foremost soloists. In Graz he took the principal chair of the university orchestra. Since 2026 he sits at the first desk of the Camerata Strumentale Siciliana.

Shaped by the chamber discipline of Alexander Pavlovsky, founder of the Jerusalem Quartet, and of Julian Arp. In his first semester in Graz he debuted at the city's Musikverein beside his own professor and other faculty members — rare for a newcomer. He records and performs in standing formations: the Simon Bolivar String Quartet, the string trio devoted to Gideon Klein, and an album of Brazilian quartets with concertmaster and violist Ollantay Velásquez.

  • Simon Bolivar String Quartet
  • Gideon Klein · String Trio
  • Musikverein Graz · debut
  • Brazilian quartets · recording

In Tocuyito Trío — Dalina Ugarte on violin, Joseph Ávila on piano — founded in Vienna in 2023, violin, cello and piano stand in for the pulse of the cuatro, the bandola and the maracas. Featured Ensemble of the Jeunesse for the 2023–24 season, the trio premiered the staged concert Cambur Pin-Tón at the Wiener Konzerthaus, a co-production with Oorkaan directed by Caecilia Thunnissen and nominated at the YAMawards. With Ilumina Music he carries the Latin American repertoire to Europe's largest halls.

  • Tocuyito Trío · since 2023
  • Jeunesse · Featured Ensemble
  • Cambur Pin-Tón · Konzerthaus
  • Ilumina Music · since 2020

He started teaching at fifteen and never stopped. In Catania, Sicily, he spent a year as head cello teacher at a non-profit foundation bringing free music education to the city's most vulnerable districts, on the principles of José Antonio Abreu's El Sistema. He also taught violin, viola, double bass and solfège, conducted the orchestra, and built his own method for students with motor and visual disabilities.

Teaching and social action

Music as access, not as privilege.

Santo learned in a house where music was the whole foundation, and has taught since he was fifteen. In Catania he gave that back to a city where the instrument rarely arrives: a full year leading the cello class of a non-profit foundation that offers free music education as an engine of human change — José Antonio Abreu's El Sistema principles, applied in Sicily.

The work included students with motor and visual disabilities. For them he built his own method, tying technical progress to each student's emotional development — because tuning a string and gaining confidence happen, in practice, at the same time.
