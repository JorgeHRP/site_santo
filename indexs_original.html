Your teacher

Meet Santo — the cellist who's been where you want to go.

Third generation in a family of musicians, Santo started with his father, a cellist, conductor and luthier in Trujillo, Venezuela. At 15 he was principal cellist of his home state's orchestra — and giving his first lessons.

He then joined the Simón Bolívar Symphony Orchestra, studied with Julian Arp at the Kunstuniversität Graz, debuted at the Musikverein Graz and performed at the Wiener Konzerthaus.

In August 2021 he was a chamber music and stage presence mentor at the Academy for Impact through Music (AIM) summer residency in Salzburg, Austria, representing the Ilumina collective. At the in-person residency hosted by the Hilti Foundation, he guided the Ilumina group using advanced leadership and rehearsal-facilitation methods, with intensive work on ensemble playing, non-verbal communication, active listening and the stage presence of young musicians.

He teaches the way he was taught: with discipline, warmth and zero fluff. He has even developed his own method for students with motor and visual disabilities — because every student deserves a path.

Questions

Everything you're wondering.

Is the first call really free?

Yes. The 20-minute diagnostic call is free, with no card and no obligation. It's how Santo learns your level and how you find out whether you click.

Do I need to be advanced?

Not at all. Santo teaches complete beginners and pre-professionals. He began teaching at 15 and has taught cello, violin, viola, double bass and solfège.

Are online cello lessons effective?

Yes, when the teacher knows what to look for. Santo works on posture, bow, left hand and sound over video, and uses recording feedback between lessons so progress doesn't stop when the call ends.

What languages do you teach in?

Spanish, English, Portuguese, Italian and German. Tell him your preference in the form.

I'm preparing for an audition or exam. Can you help?

That's one of the best uses of the diagnostic call. Santo has led sections in professional orchestras and can build a focused preparation plan around your date.

What time zones do you cover?

Santo is based in Sicily (CET). Lessons work for Europe, the Americas and beyond — mornings and evenings are the easiest overlap for the US and Latin America.

What happens after I send the form?

He replies personally, usually within 24 hours, to schedule your free call at a time that suits you.
